# DKeramik — marketing plans

How to become visible, and a marketing plan for each money path. Companion file: [business-plans.md](./business-plans.md) (offers, prices, capacity, risks).

Visibility for a ceramist is not a follower count. It is being easy to find when someone nearby wants clay **this month**, and easy to remember when they need a gift in **December**. One name, one mug, one studio feeling — repeated until it is boring to you.

Cadence below is a planning default for a solo studio, not a platform requirement.

---

## The visibility system (all paths share this)

Do this before path-specific campaigns.

| Layer | What to set up | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Findable** | Google Business Profile with photos, hours, WhatsApp, category Pottery / Art class. Same name as Instagram and the shop. | Workshops are local search. If you are not on the map, you do not exist for “keramika Kaunas”. |
| **Recognizable** | One hero mug, one glaze family, one backdrop. The grid should look like the same studio, not a moodboard of other people’s ceramics. | People cannot recommend what they cannot describe. |
| **Reachable** | Bio: what you sell + city + link to shop/dates. Reply in 24h. Deposits, not “maybe”. | Interest dies in DMs without a date and a price. |
| **Remembered** | Email or WhatsApp list from every student and fair buyer. Monthly note: one photo, one date, one piece for sale. | 80 people who have been in your studio outperform 8,000 cold followers. |

### This week, in order

1. Freeze the hero mug and the 2-hour workshop offer.
2. Put four dates on a graphic and in a Google Business post.
3. Photograph eight real pieces and list them in the shop.
4. Ask five people you already know to book or to buy a voucher.
5. Apply to one design-leaning autumn date and one Christmas date.

Everything else waits.

## Ideas that fit DKeramik

Brand: ritual, home, handmade warmth. (“Homes become true homes when they are filled with heartfelt details.”)

| Idea | How to run it | Feeds which path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open studio, last Saturday | 2 hours, tea, 10 pieces on a table, no pressure to buy. Invite students and neighbors. One reel from the table. | Tableware, workshops, fairs |
| Coffee collaboration | Leave 6 mugs at a cafe whose interior matches you. They use them. You get a tagged story. Split nothing unless they sell. | Commissions, tableware |
| Florist table | Your vase + their Saturday bouquet, photographed once. Cross-Stories. Sell the vase through you. | Tableware, fairs |
| Gift voucher as the product | Sell the workshop before you sell the pot. Mother’s Day, birthdays, Christmas. Physical card + PDF. | Workshops |
| Pickup as content | When students collect work, 3 photos with permission. That clip books the next date. | Workshops |
| Pinterest as a quiet shop window | Vertical photos of ware in use, link to the piece. 20 minutes on Sundays. | Tableware |
| Local press pitch | 5-sentence email to a local journalist: studio, one fair, one workshop date, one photo. Once per season. | All |
| Same URL everywhere | If the public still lands on a long GitHub Pages URL, point dkeramik.lt at the shop. Email already uses that name. | All |

## Weekly rhythm (when you are doing the stack)

**30–45 minutes a day.** Stack = workshops + capsule + two fairs. See [business-plans.md](./business-plans.md#recommendation-a-stack-not-six-businesses).

| Day | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Kiln or making clip. Reply to every comment and DM. |
| Tue | One still photo of the hero mug in use. Update Stories highlights (Dates / Shop / Studio). |
| Wed | Workshop reminder or seat count. Facebook Event if a date is inside 12 days. |
| Thu | Process carousel or journal paragraph. Batch replies. |
| Fri | Partner or student repost. Pack orders. |
| Sat | If teaching or open studio: film 20 seconds. If not: restock photos. |
| Sun | Plan next week’s 3 clips. Add new emails to one list. No new product ideas. |

### Skip for now

Etsy as a second shop, TikTok as a career, a new logo, a third clay body, daily vlogging, paid ads on cold audiences, every *mugė* in the country.

### Protect

Kiln calendar, drying space, November production, your name spelled one way, prices that can rise later.

### Measure monthly

Cash in, sessions run, pieces finished, emails collected, fair net vs fee. **Followers last.**

## 1. Studio workshops

**Promise:** Sell a feeling (slow evening, warm studio, a cup they made) more than a technique. People buy the date, not the clay body.

Business plan: [business-plans.md — Studio workshops](./business-plans.md#1-studio-workshops).

### Channels

- Instagram + Facebook Events (still how city groups hear about workshops)
- Google Business Profile: *keramikos dirbtuvės* / pottery class + city
- Gift vouchers sold on the DKeramik shop and at fairs
- Partners: one florist, one coffee shop, one yoga/pilates studio with a poster and a story swap
- After each class: email/WhatsApp for pickup + 15% off a finished DKeramik mug

### What to publish

- Reel: hands centering clay, 8–12 seconds, Lithuanian caption, date in the first line
- Carousel: 5 photos of past pieces + “what is included”
- Story series: 3 remaining seats, then 1 seat, then sold out
- Student permission photos on pickup day — this is your best ad

### Cadence

3 short videos per week, daily Stories when a date is open, one Facebook Event per date created 12 days ahead, one partner shout per month.

### Budget

**€0–40/month.** Boost only the one reel that already got saves, geo-targeted to the city ±40 km, ages 25–45.

### Success looks like

- Each public date at 4+ paid seats 10 days before
- Deposit rate ≥ 50% of ticket
- 30% of students tagged you or sent a photo
- Email/phone list grows by 8–12 people per month

### 8-week calendar

| When | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Offer text, 4 dates, deposit method, Google Business, 15-photo phone shoot. |
| Week 2 | Publish date 1, partner poster, 3 reels, Facebook Event. |
| Week 3 | Fill leftover seats via Stories and two local Facebook groups (one post, not spam). |
| Week 4 | Run session 1, collect photos, sell 2 vouchers. |
| Weeks 5–6 | Session 2, announce course waitlist. |
| Weeks 7–8 | Sessions 3–4, Christmas voucher graphic, ask 5 students for a one-line review. |

## 2. Signature tableware + shop

**Promise:** The product is the ad. One recognizable mug, used in real kitchens, repeated until people can spot DKeramik across a room.

Business plan: [business-plans.md — Signature tableware](./business-plans.md#2-signature-tableware--shop).

### Channels

- Own shop (already live) — primary checkout, invoices, Paysera
- Instagram Shop-style grid: same mug, many lives
- Pinterest (ceramics and interior boards still send quiet traffic)
- Journal on the site: one process post per month
- Packaging: card with Instagram and care, every box

### What to publish

- In-use: oatmeal, berries, coffee — this matches the DKeramik story you already wrote
- Kiln-opening reel, then cut to the mug in a kitchen
- “How this was made” 6-slide carousel
- Gift guide in November: 3 price points

### Cadence

2 product posts + 2 process videos per week. Restock Stories whenever admin stock changes. One journal entry per month.

### Budget

**€0–80 once** for a cheap roll of backdrop paper and a daylight bulb. No ads until the hero mug has 10 organic sales.

### Success looks like

- 10 pieces listed with real photos within 30 days
- First 5 shop orders (pickup is fine)
- Repeat glaze/form so followers recognize the line
- Breakage in shipping = 0 on first 10 parcels

### 8-week calendar

| When | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Freeze 3 forms, throw the first dozen. |
| Week 2 | Photo day, update product pages, set prices in admin. |
| Week 3 | Announce “now in the shop” with 3 Stories/day for 4 days. |
| Week 4 | First pickup/ship, ask for a kitchen photo. |
| Weeks 5–8 | Second batch, two-mug set, start a simple waitlist for restocks. |

## 3. Curated fairs and pop-ups

**Promise:** Fairs are not “being present”. They are a launch. Treat each one like a product drop: announce, countdown, recap, restock online.

Business plan: [business-plans.md — Fairs](./business-plans.md#3-curated-fairs-and-pop-ups).

### Channels

- Instagram countdown starting 10 days out
- Email/WhatsApp to workshop students: “I’ll be at…”
- Organizer tags — send them 5 good photos the week before
- Stories from the stall (process of wrapping, not empty table)
- Monday after: sold pieces, remaining pieces in the shop, thank-you

### What to publish

- Reel: packing the crate, then the stall in 5 seconds
- Map/pin and hours in the first Story frame
- One “under €25 / under €40 / gift set” graphic

### Cadence

Silence until 10 days before, then daily until the event, then a 3-day recap. Do not post stall photos of other makers without permission.

### Budget

Stall fee + **€0 ads**. Organizers already paid for footfall.

### Success looks like

- Net ≥ 3× stall fee
- 10 new Instagram follows per day of fair
- 8 workshop voucher conversations
- Email or follow from 20 people

### 8-week calendar

| When | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | Apply and pay deposits. |
| Weeks 3–5 | Produce specifically for the stall (the capsule, not experiments). |
| Week 6 | Display rehearsal at home, photograph it. |
| Week 7 | Announce. |
| Week 8 | Event, then list leftovers the same night. |

## 4. Cafes, interiors, private sets

**Promise:** Show the work in rooms, not on a white infinity sweep. Owners buy atmosphere.

Business plan: [business-plans.md — Commissions](./business-plans.md#4-cafes-interiors-private-sets).

Do not run a weekly public content stream just for this. Not the main bet for the next 90 days.

### Channels

- In-person samples
- Instagram tags of interiors + a calm DM after a like/comment from a venue
- Interior designers’ Facebook groups and one breakfast with a stylist
- Leave-behind mug at a friendly cafe (only if they will use it, not hide it)

### What to publish

- Before/after: empty table vs. table with your ware
- Short note: “24 mugs, 10 weeks, this glaze”
- Client-ready PDF, one page, Lithuanian

### Cadence

2 venue visits per month once samples exist.

### Budget

**€0–60** for samples. Do not run ads targeting restaurants.

### Success looks like

- 8 conversations
- 2 sample leave-behinds
- 1 paid deposit in a year is a win at this stage

### Calendar

Do not schedule this in the first 8 weeks. When the capsule exists: one week for the line sheet, one week of 4 visits, then stop and return to production.

## 5. Corporate and wedding gifts

**Promise:** Make buying easy for a busy person: one product, one deadline, one invoice.

Business plan: [business-plans.md — Corporate and wedding gifts](./business-plans.md#5-corporate-and-wedding-gifts).

### Channels

- Warm WhatsApp/email to people you already know
- LinkedIn once, in Lithuanian, with the box photo
- Wedding planners and florist partners
- Fair sign: “company gifts — ask for the PDF”

### What to publish

- Photo of 8 identical boxes in a row (signals you can repeat)
- Deadline graphic
- Unboxing 10-second video

### Cadence

Silence until **1 October**, then 3 posts/week until the deadline, then stop.

### Budget

**€0.** Maybe €20 to boost the box photo in the city in late October.

### Success looks like

- 15 PDFs sent
- 2 companies quote
- 1 paid order ≥ 8 boxes

### Calendar

| When | Work |
| --- | --- |
| September | Box design, PDF, 8 extra mugs as buffer. |
| October | Outreach weeks. |
| November | Produce and invoice. |

Do not invent this in the first month of studio season.

## 6. Boutiques and consignment

**Promise:** The shop’s audience is the point. Negotiate a tagged post when they unpack, not only a shelf.

Business plan: [business-plans.md — Boutiques](./business-plans.md#6-boutiques-and-consignment).

### Channels

- In-person buying appointments
- Shop’s Instagram (supply photos they can post)
- Your grid: “now at [shop]” with a map

### What to publish

- Shelf photo in situ
- Restock Stories
- Joint reel with the florist if they will film 20 seconds

### Cadence

1 visit cycle per month. One public “stockist” post when a shop goes live.

### Budget

**€0.** Do not pay for shelf placement.

### Success looks like

- 3 serious conversations
- 1 stockist
- Sell-through ≥ 40% in 60 days or take the work back

### Calendar

After 20 finished capsule pieces: one week of visits, then production to refill the studio first.
